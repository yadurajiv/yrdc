Services

Four things I get asked for most. If what you need isn’t one of them, say so and we’ll work it out.

01

Design and UX Audit

A structured review of a build, prototype, onboarding flow, feature, tool or player journey. You get a clear read on what is working, what is confusing, and what to fix first.

It also runs as a pitch or demo review, ahead of a publisher meeting, investor update, grant application, milestone or public showcase.

What you get

  • Recorded playthrough or walkthrough
  • Design, UX and systems notes
  • Prioritised fix list and opportunity map
  • A call to talk it through
02

Prototype Sprint

Turn an early mechanic, tool, learning loop or interaction model into something playable and testable, before the team commits production time to it.

Useful when an idea has been argued about in documents for longer than it would take to build.

What you get

  • Playable prototype or interaction mock
  • Source and design notes
  • The assumptions it tested, and what it answered
  • Next-step production recommendations
03

Ongoing Design Support

Senior design direction on a monthly retainer, sometimes called a fractional design lead. For teams that need critique, prioritisation and momentum without hiring full-time.

This is the closest thing to having me on the team. It works best when someone internal owns the day-to-day and needs a second senior head on the hard calls.

What you get

  • Weekly design review
  • Systems, UX and feature direction
  • Mentorship for the design team
  • Documentation support
04
