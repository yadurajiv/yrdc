        ▦ SuperGrid v0.3.1

        A multi-stream video wall for watching many things at once — news, live cams, music, streams — in one grid, with one tap to move the sound around. Vanilla HTML/JS/CSS, no backend, everything saved in your browser.

        How to use it

        • Add streams — ＋ Add or the quick switcher (s): search your channels, filter by #tag, or paste any URL (YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Vimeo, a direct video, or a webpage).
        • Give a tile sound — click it; everything else mutes. Click again to silence. For more than one at once, click a tile's 🔊 icon (in its bar) to unmute it on its own. Each tile has its own volume slider; the slider up top is the global master.
        • Expand — double-click a tile (or Shift+number). Esc restores.
        • Tile controls (hover a tile): play/pause, loop, speed, 🖱 interact, ↻ reload if it freezes, 🔗 copy link, ⌕ swap, expand, close.
        • Saved grids — arrange tiles, then ▥ Grids (p) to save the whole layout under a name and switch between setups. Each grid has its own share link.
        • Everything auto-saves and restores on reload. Press ? for all shortcuts.

        Changelog

        v0.3.1 — 26 Jul 2026

        • Fixed the master-volume slider snapping to mute when you released a drag; mute now lives on the 🔊 icon.
        • Cache-busting: CSS/JS are versioned so a new deploy never leaves you on stale files; added no-store + Expires headers (.htaccess / _headers) for hosting.

        v0.3 — 25 Jul 2026

        • Per-tile volume — each tile has its own volume slider; the top slider is now a global master.
        • Multi-audio — click a tile's 🔊 icon to unmute it without silencing the others; clicking the tile body still solos.
        • Auto-replay — the loop button now replays YouTube videos at the end too (not just local files).
        • Playlists — YouTube playlist tiles get ⏮ / ⏭ next & previous buttons.
        • No fixed tile limit — add as many as you like (number keys address the first 9).

        v0.2 — 22 Jul 2026

        • Saved grids: name & store whole layouts, reload anytime, per-grid share links.
        • Master volume slider, with reliable one-tile-at-a-time audio and muted autoplay.
        • Per-tile play/pause (now reflects the player's real state), reload (↻) and copy link (🔗).
        • Pasted YouTube/Vimeo links auto-resolve to the real video title.
        • 🖱 Interact button now works for Twitch, Kick and Vimeo — not just YouTube.
        • YouTube default channels use direct watch?v= links (channel-live embeds stopped rendering).
        • Hidden-UI tiles stay interactive on hover; embed hint only shows when a page is actually blocked.
        • No-cache local server so edits always show without a hard refresh.

        v0.1 — initial

        • Multi-stream grid with layouts (auto, 2×2, 3×3, row, focus) and click-to-focus audio.
        • Quick switcher, channel manager with tags, opened-URL history.
        • Sources: YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Vimeo, HLS, DASH, direct media, local files.
        • Share links and localStorage persistence.
